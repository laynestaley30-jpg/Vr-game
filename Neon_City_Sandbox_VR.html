Neon City Sandbox

Checking for VR…

VR: left stick walks, right stick turns. Trigger places a block, grip removes one. Point at a floating color swatch (it follows you) to pick it. The dark sphere clears all blocks. At the armory table, grip picks up or drops a weapon; trigger fires a gun or swings a melee weapon and breaks the block you hit. The orange sphere (or key M) switches between the city and the arena.

Desktop: click places, right-click removes, drag looks, WASD moves, 1–6 picks color.
